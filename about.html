About

Making smart contract security accessible to everyone.

AuditWise exists because great security tools shouldn't require a full-time security team to interpret.

The problem

Static analysis tools like Slither, Mythril, and Aderyn are incredibly powerful. They scan your code and produce detailed output covering every potential vulnerability — from reentrancy to integer overflows, from access control issues to gas optimizations.

But that output is dense. It's written for machines, not humans. A single scan can produce dozens of findings with cryptic rule IDs, code references, and severity flags that require a security expert to interpret.

For solo developers, small teams, and auditors without a full-time security department, this creates a painful gap: you know there are issues, but you don't have the time or expertise to triage them properly.

Our approach

AuditWise takes the raw output from your existing scanners and turns it into a report a human can actually act on.

We don't replace your tools. We don't run our own scans. We simply translate — taking the deterministic findings your tools already produce and adding the layer of interpretation that makes them useful.

Every finding gets a plain-English explanation, a clear "why this matters" section, and a recommended fix. Everything is ranked by severity so you know exactly what to look at first.

How it's built

AuditWise is built on a small, focused stack chosen for reliability and transparency:

Supabase

For authentication, database, and backend edge functions. Handles everything from user accounts to report storage.

Groq

For fast LLM inference. Powers the interpretation layer that turns raw tool output into human-readable findings.

Paddle

For payments. Handles checkout, tax, and compliance so we can focus on the product.

Resend

For email delivery. Sends you a link to your report the moment it's generated.

Netlify

For hosting the frontend. Fast, global, and simple to deploy.

What we believe

Deterministic first, AI second.

The findings come from your tools. The AI only interprets them. This means no hallucinated vulnerabilities.

Transparency over magic.

The raw scan output is always available in your report. You can always verify what we built on top of.

Not a replacement for real audits.

AuditWise is a triage tool. It helps you prioritize. It does not replace a professional security audit — especially before deploying high-value contracts.

Simple pricing.

One price, one scan, one report. No subscriptions, no lock-in, no hidden fees.
