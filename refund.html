Refund Policy

Last updated:

We want you to be satisfied with every scan you purchase. This policy explains when we issue refunds and how to request one.

1. Summary

We refund when:

  • Your scan failed to produce a report due to a technical error on our side.
  • Your report was delivered but is materially incomplete or unreadable due to a bug.
  • You were charged twice for the same scan.
  • You requested a refund within 7 days and have not accessed the report.

We do not refund when:

  • You simply disagreed with the findings.
  • The scan ran successfully but identified fewer issues than you expected.
  • You submitted scan output that was malformed or unrelated to your code.
  • You accessed the report and later changed your mind.

2. How to request a refund

Email us at support@auditwise.dev with:

  • The email address on your AuditWise account.
  • The Paddle transaction ID (found in your Paddle receipt email).
  • A short description of the issue.

We aim to respond to refund requests within 2 business days.

3. Timeframe

Refund requests must be made within 7 days of purchase. After 7 days, refunds are issued only at our discretion, and generally only in cases of verified technical failure.

4. How refunds are processed

Refunds are processed through Paddle, our merchant of record. Once approved, the refund is issued to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take 5–10 business days for the funds to appear.

5. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge was made in error, please contact us first at support@auditwise.dev before initiating a chargeback with your bank. We resolve most issues quickly and directly. Accounts with unresolved chargebacks may be suspended.

6. Free trial scans

AuditWise does not currently offer free trial scans. All scans are paid. If you're unsure whether AuditWise fits your needs, contact us first — we're happy to answer questions before you purchase.

7. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any statutory rights you may have under the consumer protection laws of your jurisdiction. Where local law provides stronger refund rights than this policy, local law prevails.

8. Contact

For refund questions, contact us at support@auditwise.dev or through our contact page.
