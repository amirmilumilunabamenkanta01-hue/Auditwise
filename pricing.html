Pricing questions

Do I have to pay before I see if it works?

You pay per scan. Each scan produces one full report. If the report doesn't parse correctly, you can request a refund — see our refund policy.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay $29 each time you run a scan. No monthly charge, no auto-renewal.

Can I use this for client work?

Yes. The reports are yours to use in client deliverables, internal reviews, or public disclosures.

What if my scan is larger than 150,000 characters?

Trim your scan to just the findings section (usually the top of the output) before submitting. Most standard scans fit well within the limit.
