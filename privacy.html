Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how AuditWise ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our service. By using AuditWise, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

AuditWise provides AI-assisted security report generation for smart contract code. Our service analyzes raw output from third-party static analysis tools and produces a human-readable security report.

2. Information we collect

When you use AuditWise, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored in hashed form). We use this to authenticate you.
  • Profile information: display name and company name, if you choose to provide them.
  • Scan content: the smart contract scan output you submit. This is stored so we can generate the report and so you can access it later.
  • Report data: the security report generated from your scan.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by Paddle. We do not see or store your credit card details.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, and basic usage data for security and rate-limiting purposes.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and provide access to your account.
  • Generate and store your security reports.
  • Send you a link to your report via email.
  • Process payments through our payment provider (Paddle).
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

We rely on the following third-party providers to operate the service. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend hosting.
  • Groq — large language model inference used to generate report content.
  • Paddle — payment processing and tax compliance.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Netlify — frontend hosting.

5. Data retention

We retain your account information and reports for as long as your account is active. Raw scan output is automatically deleted 30 days after report generation. When you delete your account, your profile, reports, and associated data are permanently deleted.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data (available in your dashboard).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@auditwise.dev.

7. Cookies and tracking

AuditWise uses only essential cookies required for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and access controls. No system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

AuditWise is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. International transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. By using AuditWise, you consent to these transfers. Our providers (Supabase, Paddle, Groq, Resend, Netlify) operate globally and maintain appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of AuditWise after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@auditwise.dev or through our contact page.
